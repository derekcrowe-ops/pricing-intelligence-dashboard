04 / STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS

Where the opportunity may be

Research observations are separated from interpretations and hypotheses for the venture.

INTERPRETATION · NOT VALIDATION
↗
OBSERVED PRODUCT GAP

Bring gear and sticks together

Observed: The sampled ice-hockey gear bags and stick bags are separate products. No comparable integrated-holder ice-hockey gear bag was verified.

Interpretation: A combined bag could address the venture’s one-trip convenience idea. Test demand and practical trade-offs with hockey families.

01 / 03
◌
PRICE SPACE

Test a modest step above basic

Observed: BAUER’s carry bag is $109.99; Pacific Rink’s junior bag is $195.99. The Mission 1 range of $119–$149 sits between those offers.

Interpretation: A small convenience premium may be testable if the integrated holders are secure and do not compromise comfort.

02 / 03
⌂
BUYER HYPOTHESIS

Speak to the rink-run parent

Workspace insight: The student’s prior review analysis identifies carrying comfort as a pain point and suggests parents often buy the bag.

Next question: Interview parents about two-stick capacity, carrying method, durability, and acceptable price before making product claims.

EVIDENCE TRACE

How to read this dashboard

Scope and calculation notes

METHOD
01

Comparable benchmark. USD prices for complete ice-hockey equipment bags only: BAUER Premium Carry Bag S24 and Pacific Rink Junior and Player bags. Stick-only products and TK’s EUR field-hockey duffle are excluded.

02

Representative offers. One designated offer per competitor where a relevant product was researched. The CCM and Warrior representatives are separate stick bags, and TK is a different sport; neither is in the complete-bag price statistics.

03

Statistics. Minimum, maximum, average, and median are calculated from the three comparable complete bag prices. The offer table shows the full six-offer set, including unlike currencies and products without mixing them.

04

Price-position rule. The selected price is compared to the observed minimum, median, and maximum of that three-offer set. “Lower half” and “premium half” describe sides of the sample median, not consumer segments.

!
Research limits

Small selected sample; prices may have changed; availability was not consistently recorded. No directly comparable integrated-holder ice-hockey bag price was verified. No currency conversion or demand analysis is included.
